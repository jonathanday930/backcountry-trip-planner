# Curated backpacking route research

This catalog is the first route-data pass for every currently registered
backcountry destination. The machine-readable files live beside each park's
`campsites.json` as `known-routes.json`. Each route's `stops` is the ordered
complete list of permit/campsite choices encountered along the corridor;
`routeStops`, when present, is the smaller geometry corridor used to calculate
distance and elevation. That distinction matters for clustered sites: routing
through all 17 Boy Scout sites would incorrectly turn an 8-mile trail into a
long zig-zag.

Totals below are generated by the app's OSM/DEM engine on 2026-09-02. They are
planning values, not survey-grade facts; published mileage often differs due to
trail reroutes, side trips, GPS tracks, and whether a report counts a shuttle
road. Permit-area/zone routes should never be read as a promise of a fixed tent
pad.

## Sources and interpretation

- [Yellowstone NPS backcountry planning](https://www.nps.gov/yell/planyourvisit/backcountryhiking.htm) documents 1,114 trail miles, 293 designated backcountry camps, permit limits, river crossings, and seasonal pass conditions. Trip-report cross-checks included [Backcountry Post's Bechler reports](https://backcountrypost.com/threads/bechler-6.12560/) and [Reddit's Yellowstone route discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/yellowstone/comments/1avnjy/).
- Grand Canyon's route names and use areas come from the [NPS backcountry trails page](https://home.nps.gov/grca/planyourvisit/campsite-information.htm), [trail-distance tables](https://home.nps.gov/grca/planyourvisit/trail-distances.htm), [Tonto distances](https://www.nps.gov/grca/planyourvisit/upload/tonto_distances.pdf), and [Boucher Trail sheet](https://www.nps.gov/grca/planyourvisit/upload/boucher_trail.pdf). The [Royal Arch guide](https://www.halfwayanywhere.com/united-states/arizona/royal-arch-loop-ultimate-backpacking-guide/) and Grand Canyon trip reports were used as a sanity check on route naming, but Royal Arch is not included because the current app data lacks a reliable mapped campsite corridor.
- [Mount Rainier's NPS Wonderland page](https://www.nps.gov/mora/planyourvisit/the-wonderland-trail.htm) supplies camp-to-camp mileages and says the loop is about 93 miles/27,000 feet each way; [Northern Loop NPS](https://home.nps.gov/mora/planyourvisit/northern-loop.htm) supplies the 8,500-foot gain. [Where the Road Forks' campsite guide](https://wheretheroadforks.com/wonderland-trail-wilderness-campsites/) and [Halfway Anywhere](https://www.halfwayanywhere.com/trails/wonderland-trail/wonderland-trail-hiking-guide/) were used to check campsite naming.
- North Cascades route names and camps come from the [NPS loop recommendations](https://home.nps.gov/noca/planyourvisit/loop-backpacks.htm) and [Wilderness Trip Planner](https://www.nps.gov/noca/planyourvisit/wilderness-trip-planner.htm). Trip-report checks included [Trillium Treks' Devil's Dome report](https://trilliumtreks.com/trip-reports/devils-dome-loop) and [WTA Rainbow–McAlester reports](https://www.wta.org/go-hiking/trip-reports/trip_report.2022-10-02.4332323364).
- Zion's named camps and numbered corridors come from the [NPS La Verkin Creek page](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/laverkin-creek-trail.htm) and [Zion Wilderness Guide](https://www.nps.gov/zion/learn/news/upload/Zion_Wilderness_Guide_2021web.pdf). Mileage/elevation was cross-checked with [CleverHiker's Zion Traverse guide](https://www.cleverhiker.com/backpacking/zion-traverse-backpacking-guide/) and [Backpacking Routes](https://backpackingroutes.com/zion-traverse/).
- Sequoia–Kings Canyon route names and headline distances come from the [NPS Rae Lakes page](https://www.nps.gov/seki/planyourvisit/rae-lakes-loop.htm) and [High Sierra Trail page](https://www.nps.gov/seki/planyourvisit/high-sierra-trail.htm). [Somewhere Sierra's Mineral King report](https://somewheresierra.com/backpacking-mineral-king/) and [Backpacking Routes' Big SEKI description](https://backpackingroutes.com/big-seki-sequoia-kings-canyon-loop/) supplied itinerary/campsite cross-checks. The current export has quota-entry points, not a complete tent-site inventory.
- Joshua Tree's complete Boy Scout site list and coordinates come from the [NPS Boy Scout Trail Zone page](https://www.nps.gov/jotr/planyourvisit/the-boy-scout-trail-zone.htm); the [NPS management-plan appendix](https://www.nps.gov/jotr/learn/management/upload/Appendix_D_2_1.pdf) gives CRHT (37 miles) and Boy Scout (8 miles). Trip-report checks included [Trekking the Planet](https://www.trekkingtheplanet.com/our-adventures/ttpnps/a-joshua-tree-national-park-traverse-trekking-the-planet) and [This Will Be Our Gear](https://www.thiswillbeourgear.com/trip-report-the-boy-scout-trail-the-maze-zone-and-indian-cove-at-joshua-tree-national-park/).
- Rocky Mountain site names come from the [NPS designated-site table](https://home.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/site_details.htm); the North Inlet/Tonahutu loop was cross-checked against [Rocky Mountain Raider](https://rockymountainraider.blogspot.com/2012/07/north-inlet-divide-tonahutu-loop-in-rmnp.html). Grand Teton's route variants and published mileages are directly from the [NPS routes and zones page](https://www.nps.gov/grte/planyourvisit/backcountryroutesandzones.htm), with [Earth Trekkers](https://www.earthtrekkers.com/paintbrush-canyon-cascade-canyon-loop/) and [PackLite Life](https://packlitelife.com/how-to/teton-crest-trail-guide/) used as independent checks.
- Glacier's camp inventory follows the [NPS wilderness campground map](https://www.nps.gov/glac/planyourvisit/upload/Wilderness-Campground-Map-2024_accessible.pdf); the route choices were checked against NPS [Two Medicine guidance](https://home.nps.gov/glac/planyourvisit/hikingtwomedicine.htm) and trip reports discussing the North Circle and Nyack/Coal Creek corridors.
